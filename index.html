01 THE DISCIPLINE

Where Engineering Discipline Meets Fiduciary Protection.

Construction projects rarely falter on architectural blueprints; they falter in the unmonitored gaps between contractors, budget creep, and compromised site tolerances.

At SunRise PMC, we function as the owner’s unyielding advocate on the job site. Operating from Surat, Gujarat, our seasoned directors and resident site engineers introduce structural clarity, forensic bill checking, and CPM scheduling to Western India’s most prestigious developments.

01.1

Forensic Budget Control

Pre-construction item-rate BOQ analysis, continuous cost-value reconciliation, and elimination of unapproved contractor claims.

01.2

Critical Path Method (CPM) Rigor

Milestone-level sequencing and variance modeling that identifies schedule drag weeks before it can impact the project baseline.

01.3

Zero-Defect Quality Assurance

Independent third-party lab testing of rebar, cement, concrete batches, and multi-tier structural pour sign-offs.

06 THE ROADMAP

The SunRise 4-Phase Delivery Protocol.

A battle-tested methodology designed to preempt delays before ground is broken and certify perfection at handover.

PHASE 01

Inception & Feasibility

Comprehensive site review, budget parameterization, constructability check, and statutory timeline freeze.

Output: Master Baseline Model & Risk Register
PHASE 02

Tender & Pre-Construction

Inter-disciplinary clash detection across drawings, itemized BOQs, contractor prequalification, and contracts.

Output: Zero-Clash GFC & Approved Contractor SLAs
PHASE 03

Active Site Supervision

Resident engineers on-site conducting batch tests, joint measurements for contractor bills, and safety enforcement.

Output: Weekly CVR Dashboards & Certified Pour Logs
PHASE 04

Handover, Snagging & AMC

Defect snag clearance, final account closures, as-built documentation verification, and AMC facility onboarding.

Output: Zero-Defect Handover & Facility Maintenance
