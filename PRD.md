## 1. Executive Summary

SunRise PMC is Surat's leading **End-to-End Project Management Consultancy** in the construction domain. The existing website (sunrisepmc.in) is built on WordPress and lacks visual ambition, animation quality, and emotional storytelling found in world-class real estate / construction consultancy websites.

**Goal:** Rebuild SunRise PMC's website as a luxury-grade, animation-rich, immersive experience - combining the editorial dark-luxury aesthetic of Sobha Privy Collection and the wellness-residence narrative of Springs Estate - but tailored for a B2B construction PMC audience.

**Core Value Proposition:** Surat's most trusted PMC - delivering precision, transparency, and control over time, cost, quality and safety for construction projects across India.
